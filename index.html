Founder story

Building a fairer way to discover talent

Through Neuroforge Solutions, I am developing intelligent software products that add useful decision support to existing business workflows. The first product is TransferATS, a hiring platform designed to find relevant experience that traditional keyword screening can miss.

TransferATS — the under-one-minute pitch

Most hiring systems look for exact words on a résumé. That means a capable person can be rejected simply because they described the same skill differently or gained it in another industry. TransferATS looks beyond exact keywords to identify transferable skills and explain how a candidate’s experience relates to a role. It gives recruiters better context while keeping the final hiring decision with people.
